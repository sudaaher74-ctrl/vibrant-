Our shelter

UHPC protection shelters with integrated CBRN systems, for fast and discreet commissioning.

Every VAULK unit leaves the factory as a sealed, container-compatible system. No exceptional convoy, no visible construction site, no disclosure of the protected address.

Material

UHPC — a standardised material (NF P 18-470) designed for high structural resistance to impacts and long-term durability.

  • Designed structural service life of over 100 years
  • Ballistic and blast performance currently being qualified according to EN 13123-1
  • Very low-porosity concrete structure, enhanced airtightness
  • Strongly reduced internal spalling

Core module

Joints designed to ensure structural integrity and continuous CBRN sealing in multi-module configurations.

Logistics capabilities

Controlled logistics chain, from production to commissioning in less than 7 days.
